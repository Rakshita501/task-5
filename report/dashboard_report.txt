INTERACTIVE DASHBOARD DEVELOPMENT

Project: Interactive Sales Dashboard

Objective
To create an interactive dashboard that summarizes sales performance and helps users explore the data.

Tools Used
Python, Pandas, Streamlit, and Plotly.

Dataset
The sample dataset contains 12 sales orders from January to April 2026. It includes order date, product, category, region, quantity, sales, and profit.

Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for total sales, total orders, and total profit.
- A monthly sales trend chart.
- Sales comparisons by product and region.
- Interactive filters for region and category.
- A table showing the filtered orders.

Key Results
- Total sales: $17,950
- Total orders: 12
- Total profit: $3,590

Conclusion
The dashboard presents sales information in a clear and interactive format. Users can filter by region and category to explore performance and compare results.
